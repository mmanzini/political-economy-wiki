# China's Consultative Socialist Democracy

**Source:** [Altri Orienti Ep.64 — In Cina esistono elezioni: ecco come funzionano](https://podcasttranscript.ai/library/ep-64-in-cina-esistono-elezioni-ecco-come)
**Author:** Simone Pieranni (Chora Media)
**Date:** May 2026

---

## Summary

China's political system is doctrinally framed as "socialist consultative democracy" (Shehuizhuyi Xieshang Minzhu), prioritising permanent consultation guided by the CCP over electoral alternation; legitimacy is performance-based rather than procedural, with eight minor parties operating as institutionalised consultative partners and a hierarchical electoral system that combines direct local votes with indirect provincial and national selection — all filtered through ideological vetting.

## Body

### Doctrine: substantive vs procedural democracy

The official line distinguishes liberal democracy's focus on procedures (universal suffrage, competitive multiparty) from China's "substantive democracy" — legitimacy from delivered outcomes (modernisation, common prosperity, moderately prosperous society) rather than periodic electoral mandate (source: 2026-05-18-AI Podcast Transcript & Summary - In Cina esistono elezioni ecco come funzionano.md). Under Xi Jinping the doctrine of "whole-process democracy" (Quanguocheng Renmin Minzhu) was elevated to law in March 2021, asserting that popular participation covers elections, consultation, decision-making, management, and supervision — not just voting (source: 2026-05-18-AI Podcast Transcript & Summary - In Cina esistono elezioni ecco come funzionano.md).

### The eight minor parties

Most of the eight "democratic parties" were founded between the 1920s and 1940s during the resistance against Japan and the subsequent civil war, representing the "third force" — liberal intellectuals, national bourgeoisie, industrialists, left-Kuomintang dissidents (source: 2026-05-18-AI Podcast Transcript & Summary - In Cina esistono elezioni ecco come funzionano.md). In 1948 they accepted CCP leadership; in September 1949 they helped draft the provisional constitution at the first Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference (source: 2026-05-18-AI Podcast Transcript & Summary - In Cina esistono elezioni ecco come funzionano.md). The eight (source: 2026-05-18-AI Podcast Transcript & Summary - In Cina esistono elezioni ecco come funzionano.md):

1. **Revolutionary Committee of the Chinese Kuomintang** — historic KMT/Taiwan ties; legal and rural affairs
2. **China Democratic League** — intellectuals in culture, education, science, technology
3. **China National Democratic Construction Association** — manufacturing/commercial entrepreneurs; SME policy
4. **China Association for Promoting Democracy** — education, publishing, media intellectuals
5. **Chinese Peasants' and Workers' Democratic Party** — public health, medicine, environmental protection
6. **China Zhi Gong Party** — overseas-returned Chinese and their relatives
7. **Jiusan Society** — senior scientists and academics
8. **Taiwan Democratic Self-Government League** — Taiwanese residents on the mainland; opposes secessionism

Critics call them "vase flowers" (花瓶) — ornamental fixtures of a CCP-controlled system (source: 2026-05-18-AI Podcast Transcript & Summary - In Cina esistono elezioni ecco come funzionano.md). They are financially integrated into the state apparatus and members sit in the National People's Congress and the CPPCC, but police and military are exclusive CCP competence (source: 2026-05-18-AI Podcast Transcript & Summary - In Cina esistono elezioni ecco come funzionano.md).

### The United Front

The framework holding the eight parties under CCP leadership is the United Front (Tongyi Zhanxian) — a strategy Mao considered fundamental, unifying all non-Communist forces (entrepreneurs, religious leaders) under one national banner (source: 2026-05-18-AI Podcast Transcript & Summary - In Cina esistono elezioni ecco come funzionano.md).

### Hierarchical elections

Direct elections exist only at the lowest administrative levels — county, urban district, township people's congress deputies, and village committees (introduced in the 1980s to fill the vacuum left by people's communes dissolution) (source: 2026-05-18-AI Podcast Transcript & Summary - In Cina esistono elezioni ecco come funzionano.md). Citizens can nominate via *haixuan* ("sea-elections" — fishing candidates from the popular base) but elected bodies answer to the local party secretary. Provincial deputies are elected by county-level deputies; the NPC is elected by provincial assemblies and the armed forces (source: 2026-05-18-AI Podcast Transcript & Summary - In Cina esistono elezioni ecco come funzionano.md).

Elections are formally competitive: 33–100% more candidates than seats for direct elections, 20–50% more for indirect — but all candidates pass through "democratic consultation" as ideological filter (source: 2026-05-18-AI Podcast Transcript & Summary - In Cina esistono elezioni ecco come funzionano.md). The NPC is roughly two-thirds CCP members, with the remaining third drawn from the eight parties and "patriotic independents" (source: 2026-05-18-AI Podcast Transcript & Summary - In Cina esistono elezioni ecco come funzionano.md).

### The legitimacy contract

The system roots itself in a Confucian-Marxist "people as foundation" (民本) doctrine: emphasis on the rulers' responsibility for citizen welfare rather than citizens' right to change rulers via vote (source: 2026-05-18-AI Podcast Transcript & Summary - In Cina esistono elezioni ecco come funzionano.md). This works only as long as the system delivers results — performance legitimacy is conditional on administrative efficacy.

## Key Takeaways

- "Socialist consultative democracy" rejects procedural-democracy standards as universal; its legitimacy claim is output-based.
- The eight minor parties are not opposition but institutionalised consultative partners with sector-specific identities — a sophisticated cooptation architecture.
- Direct elections exist only at county/village levels; everything above is indirect and ideologically pre-filtered.
- "Whole-process democracy" (2021) reframes participation as covering all decision phases, not just voting — a doctrinal answer to liberal critiques of CCP democratic deficit.
- The system priorities subsistence and development rights over civil and political rights, planning on decade-long cycles versus electoral short-termism.
