# Dutch pension reform — the Wet toekomst pensioenen (Wtp)

**Source:** [a.s.r. — Nieuwe regels voor pensioen](https://www.asr.nl/pensioen/nieuwe-regels-voor-pensioen)
**Author:** a.s.r. (Dutch insurer, consumer explainer)

---

## Summary

The Netherlands has rewritten the rules for employer-based pensions. The old system no longer fit an era in which people live longer, change jobs more often, pause work, or start their own businesses; unions, employers and government jointly designed the new rules, codified in the Wet toekomst pensioenen (Wtp) (source: 2026-08-05-Nieuwe regels voor pensioen.md).

## Timeline and obligation

- The Wtp entered into force on 1 July 2023; every pension scheme must comply by 1 January 2028. The employer is responsible for the transition, starting by engaging a pension adviser (source: 2026-08-05-Nieuwe regels voor pensioen.md).
- Employee risk if the employer does nothing: accruing pension under the old rules from 2028 onward can trigger (potentially heavy) tax liability on the accrued pension — the explainer urges workers to push employers toward an adviser (source: 2026-08-05-Nieuwe regels voor pensioen.md).

## What stays the same

- State AOW pension continues alongside employer accrual (source: 2026-08-05-Nieuwe regels voor pensioen.md).
- Already-accrued pension does not change; once in payment, it is paid for life (source: 2026-08-05-Nieuwe regels voor pensioen.md).
- Survivor's pension (nabestaandenpensioen), where present in a scheme, likely remains — though its rules are among the areas changing in design (source: 2026-08-05-Nieuwe regels voor pensioen.md).
- Accrual usually continues during occupational disability (source: 2026-08-05-Nieuwe regels voor pensioen.md).

## What changes

What changes for an individual depends on their situation (active accruer vs survivor-pension beneficiary); the source routes readers to situation-specific pages and to mijnpensioenoverzicht.nl, the national registry showing where pension has been accrued and expected AOW (source: 2026-08-05-Nieuwe regels voor pensioen.md). The consumer page does not detail the systemic shift itself (from collective defined-benefit promises toward personal pension pots with age-independent premiums) (source: needs-verification).

## Key Takeaways

- The Wtp is the Netherlands' answer to ageing and labour-market flexibilisation: all employer schemes must be converted by 1 January 2028.
- The transition burden sits with employers, but the tax risk of inaction lands on workers — an unusual incentive design.
- Continuity anchors (AOW, accrued rights, lifelong payment) are preserved to make the conversion politically viable.
