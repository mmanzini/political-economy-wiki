# European politics digest — 16 July 2026

**Source:** [Il Post EU newsletter, 16 July 2026](https://www.ilpost.it/)
**Author:** Il Post (weekly European-politics newsletter)

---

## Summary

A five-item European roundup: Hungary's parliament moves to depose president Tamás Sulyok, Jordan Bardella returns to number-two status behind Le Pen, the Commission launches the Team Gaza reconstruction fund, the Spain-Gibraltar border fence comes down after 118 years, and Berlin's mayor pays for a blackout-era tennis match (source: 2026-07-16-Inbox - manzini.m@gmail.com.md).

## Hungary: Magyar vs Sulyok

Hungary's unicameral parliament approved a constitutional amendment to remove president Tamás Sulyok, an Orbán ally. Deposing Sulyok is central to Péter Magyar's government, which since taking office in May has been dismantling Orbán's semi-authoritarian system piece by piece; the largely ceremonial president can veto laws and so obstruct Magyar's reforms. The amendment must be signed by Sulyok himself — if he refuses, Magyar has said he would pursue impeachment for violating the Constitution, a longer process decided by a Constitutional Court whose 16 judges were all Orbán appointees (source: 2026-07-16-Inbox - manzini.m@gmail.com.md).

## France: Bardella back to number two

Since Marine Le Pen announced she will be Rassemblement National's 2027 presidential candidate (after an appeals-court ruling restored her eligibility), Jordan Bardella has had to return to deputy status — visibly unenthused. He had spent a year preparing his own presidential run, with slogans, posters, an October book launch, and a deliberate repositioning toward the traditional liberal right to court moderate conservatives. Le Pen, aware of his popularity, says she would name him prime minister if she wins — though he would still need parliamentary backing (source: 2026-07-16-Inbox - manzini.m@gmail.com.md).

## The EU's Gaza reconstruction fund

The Commission presented Team Gaza, an €883.6M plan to restore essential services destroyed by Israel in the Strip (water, sanitation, rubble removal) — a first attempt to implement the UN Security Council plan approved last November, but far short of the €70B+ the EU and UN estimate reconstruction requires. Jared Kushner attended the launch, read as a win for the Commission's bid to coordinate reconstruction actors, including Trump's practically unfunded Board of Peace. The fund is financed by 12 European countries (including Italy), Japan, the Commission, the EIB and the World Bank (source: 2026-07-16-Inbox - manzini.m@gmail.com.md).

## Gibraltar: a border removed after 118 years

Schengen-area citizens can now cross freely between Spain and Gibraltar. The "Verja" fence, erected by Spain in 1908, closed entirely under Franco and only gradually reopened from 1982, has been removed under a post-Brexit agreement that de facto integrates Gibraltar (population 40,000; 96% voted Remain in 2016) into the EU customs union and Schengen. UK arrivals by air or sea still face standard Schengen entry checks. The deal also introduces a ~15% transaction tax (akin to EU VAT) that could hit Gibraltar's VAT-free luxury-shopping tourism; Spain's concern had been its 15,000 daily cross-border workers (source: 2026-07-16-Inbox - manzini.m@gmail.com.md).

## Berlin: Wegner won't run again

Berlin mayor Kai Wegner (CDU) announced he will not stand in the 20 September city elections, unable to recover from his handling of January's blackout that left ~50,000 homes without power and heating for four days — during which he claimed to have spent the first day coordinating by phone, then admitted he had also played tennis with his partner. The CDU has lost 17 points in polls since the 2023 federal elections (source: 2026-07-16-Inbox - manzini.m@gmail.com.md).

## In brief

The Commission is presenting an ETS reform that ten states, including Italy, want weakened; France's National Assembly approved an assisted-suicide law (one step from entry into force); EU foreign ministers failed to agree sanctions on illegal Israeli settlements in the West Bank; the Council approved new air-passenger protections; and Nigel Farage faces a campaign opponent wearing a bin on his head (source: 2026-07-16-Inbox - manzini.m@gmail.com.md).

## Key Takeaways

- Magyar's de-Orbánisation has reached the presidency itself, with the Orbán-appointed Constitutional Court as the likely decisive battleground.
- Le Pen's restored candidacy demotes Bardella and re-centres RN's 2027 bid on her, with Bardella as PM-designate bait for his younger electorate.
- Team Gaza (€883.6M vs €70B needed) is symbolic money spent to buy the EU a coordinator's seat in Gaza reconstruction diplomacy.
- Gibraltar's fence removal is Brexit's quiet counter-current: a British territory effectively folded into Schengen and the customs union.
