# OpenAI Presence — enterprise agents as a deployed product

**Source:** [Introducing OpenAI Presence](https://openai.com/index/introducing-openai-presence/)
**Author:** OpenAI (published 27 July 2026)

---

## Summary

OpenAI introduced Presence, an enterprise product for deploying AI agents in production across customer and internal workflows — answering questions, resolving issues, using company systems, taking approved actions, and escalating to humans. The pitch: the challenge is no longer proving agents work but making them reliable enough for high-value production work, which requires systems, evaluations and deployment expertise beyond the model itself. (Note: this article dedupes two clippings of the same URL captured 29 July and 4 August; content is identical apart from added illustrations.) (source: 2026-07-29-Introducing OpenAI Presence.md) (source: 2026-08-04-Introducing OpenAI Presence.md)

## How it works

- Each deployment starts with one job (billing issues, insurance claims, IT service requests); the agent gets only the knowledge and system access that job requires, and the company sets policies for what it can do, when it needs approval, and when a person takes over (source: 2026-07-29-Introducing OpenAI Presence.md).
- Components: policies and SOPs, guardrails, approved actions, simulations, evaluation tools, and a Codex-powered improvement loop — production sessions and escalations reveal gaps, Codex (via a Presence plugin) proposes updates, teams test against the production version and approve controlled rollouts (source: 2026-07-29-Introducing OpenAI Presence.md).
- Deployments are led by OpenAI Forward Deployed Engineers and select systems integrators under a limited GA programme; Presence is not self-serve. Insights from every deployment feed OpenAI research (source: 2026-07-29-Introducing OpenAI Presence.md).

## Proof points and partners

- Presence powers OpenAI's own English-language phone support (1-888-GPT-0090): within weeks it met or exceeded OpenAI's human frontline-quality benchmarks and now resolves 75% of inbound issues without human assistance; the Codex loop cut human handoffs by 15 percentage points in 10 days (source: 2026-07-29-Introducing OpenAI Presence.md).
- Design partners: BBVA exploring voice support for everyday banking in Mexico, SoftBank testing Japanese-language customer conversations, IAG exploring surge support during events like severe weather (source: 2026-07-29-Introducing OpenAI Presence.md).

## Key Takeaways

- Presence formalises the forward-deployed-engineer model — the Palantir playbook — as an OpenAI product line, moving the lab decisively into services revenue.
- The Codex-powered self-improvement loop is the differentiator claim: agents that adapt post-launch under human-approved change control.
- Voice-first customer support at 75% autonomous resolution is a direct labour-displacement signal for the contact-centre industry, with regulated sectors (banking via BBVA) already in scope.
