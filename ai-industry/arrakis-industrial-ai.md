# Arrakis — industrial AI out of stealth with $38M

**Source:** [Fortune exclusive, 22 July 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/07/22/arrakis-a-startup-betting-ais-biggest-payoff-is-in-industrial-sectors-not-office-work-emerges-from-stealth-with-38-million-in-venture-funding/)
**Author:** Jeremy Kahn

---

## Summary

Arrakis, a seven-month-old London- and Paris-based startup named after Dune's desert planet, emerged from stealth with $38M to build an AI "operating system" for industrial companies — aerospace, energy, logistics, manufacturing. The $30M Series A was led by Blossom Capital with Accel, GFC, MainObject and Rerail participating (Accel led the $7.5M seed); individual backers include Datadog CEO Olivier Pomel and OpenAI's Olivier Godement. Post-money valuation: $140M (source: 2026-07-29-Exclusive Startup Arrakis emerges from stealth with $38 million in funding to bring AI to industry.md).

## The thesis

- CEO Rafael Quintanilla, a former Accel VP who developed the firm's defence/industrial-resilience thesis, argues most AI investment "has targeted the 30% of workers behind a desk. The real ROI lies in the 70% running industrial operations" (source: 2026-07-29-Exclusive Startup Arrakis emerges from stealth with $38 million in funding to bring AI to industry.md).
- Competitive positioning: versus Bezos-backed Prometheus (core engineering of physical products), Arrakis wants "everything around it" — the AI layer for key operations; versus consulting giants (Accenture, BCG), software-plus-human rather than billable hours; versus Palantir — from which half his team comes, including a former head of Palantir's procurement and supply-chain team — respect, but "a 20-year-old company... a very hefty price point... a technology that is starting to become legacy" (source: 2026-07-29-Exclusive Startup Arrakis emerges from stealth with $38 million in funding to bring AI to industry.md).

## The playbook

- Start small at HQ, prove value, move to field operations on pull: for an NYSE-listed shipping company, Arrakis rebuilt operators' existing spreadsheet with AI populating data, learning and codifying operator knowledge from their corrections, to move cash-flow visibility from monthly to daily. Roughly half of fees are contingent on hitting performance targets (source: 2026-07-29-Exclusive Startup Arrakis emerges from stealth with $38 million in funding to bring AI to industry.md).
- Best traction comes from family-controlled European industrial firms — long-term thinkers who can execute top-down and sustain non-transactional relationships (source: 2026-07-29-Exclusive Startup Arrakis emerges from stealth with $38 million in funding to bring AI to industry.md).
- Model-agnostic by design, answering executives' lock-in fatigue ("everyone told us we had to be on Copilot. Then... OpenAI. Now it's Anthropic"): builds on OpenAI/Anthropic proprietary models, then migrates customers to open-source (Mistral, or Chinese vendors if permitted) wrapped in a "fat harness" claimed to deliver 2-4x quality improvement while cutting token costs ~70% (source: 2026-07-29-Exclusive Startup Arrakis emerges from stealth with $38 million in funding to bring AI to industry.md).
- Current scale: five customers, ~15 staff, plans to triple headcount and open New York and Middle East outposts (source: 2026-07-29-Exclusive Startup Arrakis emerges from stealth with $38 million in funding to bring AI to industry.md).

## Key Takeaways

- The industrial-operations layer is emerging as the contested middle ground between frontier labs, Palantir, consultancies and Prometheus-scale capital.
- The Palantir-alumni pipeline plus the "legacy" attack line place Arrakis squarely in the European challenger wave exploiting the Palantir backlash.
- Performance-contingent pricing and model-agnostic harnesses are the differentiators aimed at conservative industrial buyers wary of both consultants and lock-in.
