# Xi Jinping and Common Prosperity — From Maoist Roots to the 15th Five-Year Plan

**Source:** [Altri Orienti Ep.65 — Xi Jinping vuole una Cina meno diseguale](https://podcasttranscript.ai/library/ep-65-xi-jinping-vuole-una-cina-meno-diseguale)

**Date:** May 2026

## Summary

Xi Jinping's August 2021 speech to the Central Financial and Economic Affairs Commission elevated "common prosperity" (Gongtong Fuyu) from long-term aspiration to organising principle of the 15th Five-Year Plan, integrating it across 16 policy domains with welfare indicators displacing GDP as the primary metric — backed by the doctrine of "new quality productive forces" (innovation in AI, robotics, biotech) generating wealth to redistribute through market, fiscal, and philanthropic channels; implementation is constrained by a 2025 fiscal squeeze and global trade tensions over China Shock 2.0.

## Body

### The doctrinal lineage

The term "common prosperity" has been constant in CCP lexicon but its meaning has shifted (source: 2026-05-18-AI Podcast Transcript & Summary - Xi Jinping vuole una Cina meno diseguale.md):

- **Mao Zedong (1950s)** — collectivisation, elimination of private and feudal property; classless society via state control. Welfare systems established, but political turbulence prevented real wealth creation.
- **Deng Xiaoping (1978)** — "poverty is not socialism"; let some people and regions get rich first, with wealth trickling inland via state support and market forces. Lifted ~700-800M people from absolute poverty but produced one of the world's highest urban-rural Gini coefficients.
- **Xi Jinping (2021–)** — asymmetric growth declared superseded; common prosperity becomes urgent political task, not long-term aspiration. In 2021 in *Qiushi* Xi wrote: "Our country must firmly prevent polarisation between rich and poor, promote common prosperity, and achieve social harmony and stability" (source: 2026-05-18-AI Podcast Transcript & Summary - Xi Jinping vuole una Cina meno diseguale.md).

### The 15th Five-Year Plan architecture

Common prosperity is now the organising principle across 16 domains from governance to rural revitalisation to scientific innovation (source: 2026-05-18-AI Podcast Transcript & Summary - Xi Jinping vuole una Cina meno diseguale.md). The plan introduces 20 main indicators, 7 directly tied to public welfare — GDP is no longer the sole reference. 2030 strategic targets (source: 2026-05-18-AI Podcast Transcript & Summary - Xi Jinping vuole una Cina meno diseguale.md):

- 11.3 average years of schooling for the active population (human capital uplift)
- Life expectancy above 79 years
- Systematic expansion of childcare slots for under-3s (response to fertility crisis)
- More nursing-care beds for accelerated aging

### New quality productive forces

The doctrine of "new quality productive forces" (Xinzhi Shengchanli) frames innovation as the only sustainable redistribution mechanism — you can't redistribute a stagnant pie (source: 2026-05-18-AI Podcast Transcript & Summary - Xi Jinping vuole una Cina meno diseguale.md). Targets include applied AI, quantum information, biotech, with €120B allocated to robotics alone. The logic: innovation generates value while common prosperity ensures human capital is trained and motivated to sustain it.

### Three-tier redistribution

The campaign operates on three distribution levels (source: 2026-05-18-AI Podcast Transcript & Summary - Xi Jinping vuole una Cina meno diseguale.md):

1. **Market** — primary distribution
2. **State (fiscal/public services)** — secondary distribution
3. **Philanthropy and corporate donations** — tertiary distribution; Party actively pressures tech and industrial conglomerates

This links to the campaign against *Zibenzhuyi Fukuobazhang* — "disorderly expansion of capital." Platform-economy regulation is permanent; Chinese big tech is now expected to act as vector of common prosperity, protecting worker rights and orienting algorithms toward social utility and data sovereignty (source: 2026-05-18-AI Podcast Transcript & Summary - Xi Jinping vuole una Cina meno diseguale.md).

### Structural constraints

The "fiscal squeeze" of 2025 saw general public budget revenue decline for the first time in three decades, with real-estate market collapse drying up land-use rights sales — the backbone of local finances (source: 2026-05-18-AI Podcast Transcript & Summary - Xi Jinping vuole una Cina meno diseguale.md). Beijing's response is centralised spending and SOE transfers rather than demand stimulus.

Internationally this fuels "China Shock 2.0": massive industrial overproduction pushed by new-tech investment but constrained by weak domestic demand spills onto global markets, driving trade tensions (source: 2026-05-18-AI Podcast Transcript & Summary - Xi Jinping vuole una Cina meno diseguale.md). The contradiction: common prosperity should incentivise consumption, but policy remains supply- and production-oriented.

### Strategic framing

Common prosperity is not just domestic policy — it is a refoundation of the identity of socialism with Chinese characteristics, designed to reduce dependence on foreign markets and build an autonomous internal market capable of resisting what Xi calls "dangerous storms" in the international landscape (source: 2026-05-18-AI Podcast Transcript & Summary - Xi Jinping vuole una Cina meno diseguale.md). Success depends on translating investment into real consumer confidence.

## Key Takeaways

- Common prosperity has displaced GDP as the 15th Plan's headline metric; 7 of 20 indicators target welfare directly.
- Innovation-first redistribution: new quality productive forces (AI, quantum, biotech, €120B robotics) generate the wealth common prosperity will redistribute.
- Three-tier distribution explicitly includes philanthropy — billionaire "donations" are now policy instruments, not optional.
- 2025 fiscal squeeze (real-estate collapse, first revenue decline in 30 years) constrains state capacity to fund the welfare side of the program.
- China Shock 2.0 — overproduction without matching domestic demand — is the international cost of supply-side common prosperity.
